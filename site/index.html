A no-login Android app for Singapore drivers.

  • Live free lots at HDB, URA and mall carparks near you or where you're going, with estimated parking cost
  • EV chargers with live connector status, plug types, power and price
  • Live LTA checkpoint cameras (Woodlands, Tuas, Sentosa) and road alerts near you

  • Remember where you parked, with reminders before free parking ends or rates change
