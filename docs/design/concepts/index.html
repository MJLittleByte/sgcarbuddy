SG Car Buddy (working name) · Design concepts · Round 1 · 26 Oct 2026

Three directions, the same four screens

Each board shows Parking, EV, Roads and Weather in light and dark, at the same place (Teck Whye) with real HDB lots, real LTA camera images and a real NEA radar frame. Pick one direction, or name the parts you want to mix (for example "B's pins with A's list"). We then lock one screen and build the full wireframe from it.

A · Kerb calm and familiar

Closest to ParkUncle, but cleaner: Google-Maps-like map, white floating controls, one blue accent, standard Material 3 nav.

  • Pins: white pills with a coloured P tile and free lots.
  • List: big lots block (number, "of total", fill bar).
  • Roads: single-column camera cards, ad card after the 2nd.

B · Gantry bold and glanceable

Road-sign inspired (original, not LTA's): black plates with a yellow P, tall condensed numerals, a departure board for checkpoint times.

  • Pins: black sign plates, number coloured by availability.
  • List: board rows with a black lots tile.
  • Roads: 2-column camera wall, all 8 at once, ad row after the first two.

C · Night Drive premium, dark-first

Deep-navy map, frosted glass, glowing orbs, a floating sheet and a floating pill nav. A bright light theme is derived from it.

  • Pins: glowing orbs; the ring colour is availability.
  • List: ring gauge (how full) with free lots inside.
  • Roads: full-width camera stream with frosted captions.

The same in all three (from your plan review)

Tabs and navigation

  • Tabs renamed Parking · EV · Roads · Weather. EV uses a bolt icon, Roads a traffic-camera icon.
  • EV tab can be hidden in Settings. It is off by default unless your vehicle is electric.
  • One shared map: switching tabs never reloads it, and the place (Near you or a search) carries across.

Roads and ads

  • All 8 live LTA cameras (Woodlands ×3, Tuas ×3, Sentosa ×2) with LTA travel times to each checkpoint.
  • One in-feed ad after the 2nd camera, about the size of a camera image, clearly labelled "Ad".
  • The usual banner at the very bottom of every main screen, like SG BusXing.

Parking, Weather, Parked

  • Pins show free lots for your vehicle; the list shows walk time and est. cost for your stay.
  • Weather is cut to radar + now + next 2 hours + 4 days.
  • The Parked bar follows you on every tab while you're parked.

Recommendation: B · Gantry as the base

Why: drivers glance at this app in sunlight or in a dim carpark, and B's tall numerals and sign plates are the easiest to read. It is the most ownable look, and its camera wall shows all 8 images with one short scroll.

Possible mixes: keep B's pins, numerals and Roads wall, with A's softer white sheet and rows if B feels too heavy in daytime; or take C's ring gauge for the lots block.
