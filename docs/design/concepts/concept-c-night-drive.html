SG Car Buddy · Concept C of 3

Night Drive: dark-first, glass and glow

A premium, modern take built for evening drives. A deep-navy map, frosted glass controls, and pins as glowing orbs whose ring colour shows availability. Rows use a ring gauge (how full the carpark is) with the free lots inside. A floating pill nav and a floating sheet keep more of the map visible. Toggle to see the light version.

All concepts

What makes Night Drive different

  • Dark-first navy map, kind to eyes in the car park at night; a bright light theme is derived from it.
  • Ring gauges show how full a carpark is at a glance, with free lots inside.
  • Floating glass sheet and pill nav (icon-only, active tab labelled) for a modern, premium feel.

Review changes included

  • Tabs: Parking · EV · Roads · Weather (EV shown with a bolt; only the active tab shows its name).
  • EV tab can be hidden in Settings.
  • All 8 cameras, with one in-feed ad after the 2nd image plus the bottom banner.
  • Lean Weather: now, 2 hours, 4 days.

Trade-offs

  • Glass blur is the costliest effect on low-end Android; needs a solid fallback.
  • Icon-only inactive tabs are less learnable than labelled tabs (could show all labels).
  • Glow must not be the only cue: ring colour + number + label carry the meaning.
