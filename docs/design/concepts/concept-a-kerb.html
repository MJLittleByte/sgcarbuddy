SG Car Buddy · Concept A of 3

Kerb: calm, familiar, map first

The closest to the ParkUncle layout you liked, with the rough edges fixed. A Google-Maps-like map, white floating controls and one blue accent. Pins show free lots instead of the same "$1.20" everywhere. The list leads with a big lots block, and the bottom nav is standard Material 3. Same place, four lenses: the map never reloads between tabs.

All concepts

What makes Kerb different

  • Familiar first. Google-Maps-style colours and white floating controls, so it feels known on day one.
  • Lots block on the left of every row: big number, "of total", fill bar. The same block shows "3/3 free" on EV.
  • Standard M3 nav with a pill indicator. The safest choice for accessibility and learnability.

Review changes included

  • Tabs renamed Parking · EV · Roads · Weather. EV uses a bolt icon.
  • EV tab can be turned off in Settings (off by default unless your vehicle is electric).
  • Roads shows all 8 cameras with one in-feed ad after the 2nd image.
  • Weather is cut to radar + now + 2 hours + 4 days.

Trade-offs

  • Least distinctive of the three: it looks like a good map app, not a brand.
  • White pins can blend into light map areas. Mitigated with shadows and a coloured P tile.
