Gantry: road-sign boldness, big numbers

Inspired by gantry and carpark signs, without copying any official design. Black plates with a yellow P, tall condensed numbers that read at arm's length, and a departure-board panel for checkpoint times. The grey map lets the plates pop. The most glanceable of the three, with the strongest personality.

All concepts

What makes Gantry different

  • Tall condensed numerals (Barlow Condensed) for lots, minutes and prices: the most glanceable option.
  • Sign plates for pins, search and the parked bar give a distinctive, ownable look.
  • Camera wall: all 8 cameras in two columns, with a departure board for checkpoint times.

Review changes included

  • Tabs: Parking · EV · Roads · Weather, with a bolt for EV and a CCTV icon for Roads.
  • EV tab can be hidden in Settings.
  • One in-feed ad after the first two cameras, plus the bottom banner.
  • Lean Weather: now, next 2 hours, next days.

Trade-offs

  • Uppercase condensed text is harder to read in long names, so names stay in normal case.
  • Strong black and yellow may feel heavy to some users; the dark theme suits it best.
  • Must stay clearly original: no LTA sign shapes, colours or typefaces copied.
